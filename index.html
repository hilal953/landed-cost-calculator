Common Traps

Where Importers Lose Money

Simple mistakes in manual spreadsheets cost hundreds of dollars per order.

✈️

Air Cargo Rounds Up

If your box weighs 2.1 kg, airlines bill you for 3.0 kg. If you miss this, you undercharge your customers.

🏛️

Taxes Stack on Shipping

Customs taxes apply to the product AND the shipping fee, not just the product. Missing this causes port fee surprises.

💱

Currency Changes

You pay suppliers in foreign money like RMB, USD, or AED. If exchange rates move, your profit disappears.

📄

Typing Errors in Excel

Typing 30 items by hand into spreadsheets is slow and error-prone. One wrong number ruins your pricing.
