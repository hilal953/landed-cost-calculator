The Cost of Guessing

The Real Cost of Guessing Your Import Taxes

Excel formulas fail when real customs rules apply. Here is how profit disappears:

✈️

The Air Cargo Weight Rounding

Air cargo charges for every full kilogram. If your shipment weighs 2.1 kg, airlines charge you for 3.0 kg. Ignoring this loses you money on every unit.

📑

Customs Duties and Port Levies Compound

Customs duty, PAL, and VAT do not calculate only on the item price. They calculate on CIF and running totals. A small error in order of operations creates large unexpected bills at the port.

💱

Currency Changes Eat Your Margin

You pay suppliers in foreign currencies like RMB, USD, or AED, but sell locally. If rates change before your shipment arrives, your profit disappears.

⏱️

Manual Spreadsheet Errors

Copying 40 rows from supplier packing lists into Excel is slow and easy to mess up. One wrong decimal point can make a profitable batch look like a loss.
